# Praspel

Design by contract for PHP, written in a doc comment: what a method requires of
its arguments, what it ensures of its result, what it throws, and the domains
each value belongs to.

Taken from [`hoaproject/Praspel`](https://github.com/hoaproject/Praspel/blob/master/Grammar.pp).
Its lexer states are folded into single expressions here. Licence: New BSD.
